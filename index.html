Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can buyers from outside Maharashtra order bulk quantities?

Yes! We supply 50kg bags to full truckloads to traders, flour mills, dal mills, and stores across all states in India with transport arrangement.

How does Contract Farming work for farmers?

We provide farmers with verified seeds (especially ancient Khapli wheat) and farming advice. Before planting, we agree on a guaranteed good purchase price so you are protected even if market rates fall.

Can any farmer sell their harvested crop to Shetimay?

Yes! Any farmer can bring their harvested Khapli wheat, chana, moong, tur, soybean, bajra, or jowar. We weigh on digital scales and transfer payment directly to your bank account with zero broker cuts.

Looking to buy grains in bulk or join contract farming?

Contact us for daily wholesale rates, samples, 50kg bags to truckloads, or farmer partnership.
