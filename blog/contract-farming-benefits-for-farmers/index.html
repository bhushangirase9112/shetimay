1. The Harvest Price Crash Dilemma in Local APMC Mandis

Every Indian farmer is familiar with the heartbreaking cycle: during the crop growing season, market prices appear promising. But the moment harvest season arrives and produce floods local APMC mandis, prices crash precipitously. Middlemen and commission agents exploit distress sales, forcing hard-working farmers to sell below production cost.

Contract Farming (हमीभाव करार / Forward Purchase Agreement) with a registered Farmer Producer Company like Shetimay provides complete price protection and guaranteed market linkage. Learn more about our mission on our About Us Page.

2. Pre-Sowing Price Guarantee (हमीभाव करार)

Before sowing begins, Shetimay signs a transparent forward purchase agreement with participating farmers. We commit to a pre-agreed minimum purchase price per quintal. If market prices crash during harvest, the farmer is legally protected and receives the higher pre-agreed guaranteed price.

Furthermore, our agreements include farmer-first upside sharing provisions: if market prices rise substantially due to positive macroeconomic trends, farmers receive an upward adjustment so they never lose out on profitable market movements.

3. Certified Foundation Seed Supply & Technical Advisory

A major obstacle in cultivating heritage crops like Khapli wheat or high-protein pulses is securing authentic, unadulterated foundation seed. Shetimay supplies enrolled farmers with 100% verified, high-germination seed stock directly at the village level.

Throughout the crop cycle, our agronomy desk provides field guidance on biological seed treatment, balanced fertilization, and pest management. Farmers can read our complete agronomy instructions in the Khapli Cultivation Guide.

4. Electronic Scale Weighment & Direct Bank Transfers

Traditional mandi sales frequently involve arbitrary deductions: deductions for moisture without testing, tare weight cuts, and unpaid delays of weeks or months. Shetimay operates on complete transparency:

  • Digital Computerized Scales: Weighing conducted on certified electronic scales with printed weight receipts given to the farmer on the spot.
  • Zero Illicit Deductions: No commission fees, no dalali, and no unauthorized weight cuts.
  • Direct Bank Transfers: Payment deposited directly via NEFT/IMPS into the farmer's registered bank account within days.

5. Institutional Support under SFAC & NABARD Guidelines

The Shetimay Farmer Producer Company model operates under the institutional policy frameworks established by the Government of India:

  • Small Farmers' Agribusiness Consortium (SFAC): Fosters collective farmer bargaining power, enabling small and marginal farmers to aggregate crops and sell directly to commercial processors.
  • NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development): Provides credit linkages, post-harvest infrastructure financing, and producer organization development guidelines.
