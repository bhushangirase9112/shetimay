Khapli Wheat Cultivation Guide: Sowing Time, Seed Rate, Water Management & Yield

Shetimay Agronomy Team • October 8, 2026 • 6 min read
Key Highlights Key Facts & Summary (Quick Takeaways):
  • Optimal Sowing Window: November 1 to November 25 (cool nights trigger healthy tillering and root development).
  • Recommended Seed Rate: 40 to 45 kg per acre with 20–22.5 cm row spacing using a seed drill.
  • Irrigation Schedule: 4 to 5 critical waterings (CRI at 21 days, tillering, flowering, grain filling).
  • Yield & Assured Procurement: 12 to 15 quintals/acre with pre-agreed guaranteed purchase price by Shetimay FPC.

Introduction: Why Khapli Wheat is Highly Profitable for Farmers

With growing health consciousness and diabetic-friendly dietary trends across India, the market demand for authentic Khapli wheat (Emmer wheat) is skyrocketing. While regular hybrid wheat often faces market price crashes at harvest, pure Khapli wheat commands a steady premium price of ₹3,500 to ₹5,500+ per quintal.

Khapli is an exceptionally hardy crop, requiring less chemical fertilizer, fewer pesticide sprays, and demonstrating high natural resistance to stem rust and fungal infections. Below is Shetimay FPC's complete agronomic guide for farmers in Maharashtra and central India.

1. Soil Selection & Land Preparation

  • Ideal Soil: Deep black cotton soil (काळी कसदार जमीन) or medium loamy soil with good drainage and pH between 6.5 and 8.0.
  • Ploughing: Carry out one deep summer ploughing followed by 2–3 cross harrowings to obtain a fine, weed-free tilth.
  • Basal Organic Matter: Incorporate 4 to 5 tonnes of well-decomposed Farmyard Manure (FYM / शेणखत) or 1 tonne of vermicompost per acre during final harrowing.

2. Best Sowing Window & Seed Rate

Timing is critical for maximizing grain tillering and avoiding late terminal heat:

  • Optimum Sowing Period: November 1 to November 25 (१ ते २५ नोव्हेंबर). Early sowing benefits from cool winter nights.
  • Seed Rate: 40 to 45 kg per acre (४० ते ४५ किलो प्रति एकर) for line sowing.
  • Spacing: Row-to-row spacing of 20 to 22.5 cm and plant depth of 4 to 5 cm. Ensure seeds are placed in moist soil zone.

Seed Treatment (बीजप्रक्रिया)

Treat seeds before sowing with Trichoderma viride (5g/kg seed) to prevent root rot and seed-borne diseases, followed by Azotobacter and PSB biofertilizers (25g/kg seed) for enhanced natural nitrogen and phosphorus uptake.

3. Fertilizer & Nutrient Management

Khapli responds best to balanced organic nutrition. Excessive chemical nitrogen (urea) leads to vegetative overgrowth and crop lodging (पिक लोळणे). Recommended schedule:

  • At Sowing: 50 kg DAP + 25 kg MOP (Potash) per acre along with organic compost.
  • First Top Dressing (21–25 days): 25 kg Urea or neem-coated urea at crown root initiation.
  • Second Top Dressing (45–50 days): 20 kg Urea at active tillering stage. Micronutrient spray (Zinc + Boron) during boot stage improves grain luster.

4. Irrigation Schedule (पाणी व्यवस्थापन)

Khapli requires 4 to 5 critical irrigations depending on soil moisture retention:

  1. CRI Stage (मुकुटमुळे फुटण्याची वेळ): 20–22 days after sowing (Most critical).
  2. Tillering Stage (फुटवे फुटण्याची वेळ): 40–45 days after sowing.
  3. Flowering Stage (फुलोरा अवस्था): 60–65 days after sowing.
  4. Grain Filling Stage (दाणे भरण्याची वेळ): 80–85 days after sowing.

5. Harvest, Yield & Guaranteed Sale with Shetimay

Khapli wheat matures in 115 to 125 days. Harvest when the ears turn golden-brown and grain moisture drops below 13%. Average expected yield under good management is 12 to 16 quintals per acre.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What soil is best for cultivating Khapli wheat?

Medium to deep, well-drained black soil (Khandesh regur soil) rich in organic carbon and with neutral pH (6.8–7.8) delivers the highest grain weight and tillering.

Can we apply excess urea fertilizer to Khapli crop?

No. Excessive chemical nitrogen makes the tall Khapli stalks weak and vulnerable to lodging (falling flat) during windy periods. Use balanced basal NPK and well-rotted farmyard manure.
