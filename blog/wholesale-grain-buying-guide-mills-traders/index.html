Wholesale Grain Sourcing: How Flour Mills & Dal Mills Buy Directly from Farm Clusters

  • Procurement Cost Savings: Save 10–15% by eliminating multi-layer commission agents and APMC mandi markups.
  • Moisture Benchmark (<12%): Strictly lab-tested to prevent insect infestation, microbial heat, and storage weight losses.
  • Grading & Machine Cleaning: Sortex-cleaned or double-sieved farm-grade grain ready for chakki milling.
  • Truckload Logistics: 50kg new HDPE/jute bags with full electronic weighment slips and door-step mill delivery across India.

The Changing Landscape of Grain Sourcing for Mills & Merchants

In traditional agricultural supply chains, grain travels through multiple intermediaries: village aggregators, local commission agents (आडतिये), mandi traders, and wholesale distributors before reaching flour mills, dal mills, or modern retail brands. Each touchpoint adds a 3% to 5% commission markup, transportation inefficiencies, and quality dilution.

Today, leading millers and wholesale buyers are switching to direct farm procurement through Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) like Shetimay. Sourcing directly from Dhule and Khandesh farm clusters guarantees authenticity, stable pricing, and complete traceability.

Key Quality Parameters to Check Before Bulk Grain Purchase

1. Moisture Content Testing (ओलावा तपासणी)

Safe storage and milling efficiency depend strictly on grain moisture. For long-distance transport and warehousing, grains must test below 12.0% moisture level. High moisture leads to fungal development, grain heating, and weight loss during storage. At Shetimay, every commercial lot is tested on digital moisture meters prior to bag stitching.

2. Foreign Matter & Broken Grain Percentage

Commercial buyers need clarity on raw vs. machine-cleaned grain:

  • Raw Farm Lots (शेतकऱ्यांचा कच्चा माल): Sourced straight from electronic weighbridges after threshing. Best suited for large flour mills with in-house pre-cleaners, aspirators, and destoners.
  • Machine Cleaned & Graded (मशीन क्लीन माल): Processed through screen separators and gravity tables. Foreign matter is under 0.5%, giving zero cleaning loss for retail chakkis and packaged food brands.

3. Packaging & Electronic Weighbridge Standards

Standard packaging consists of brand-new, puncture-resistant 50kg HDPE or jute bags, machine-stitched to prevent transit spillage. Each bag is weighed on certified digital electronic scales to guarantee exact net weight.
