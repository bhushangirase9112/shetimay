Contract Farming & Farmer Services • शेतकऱ्यांच्या सेवार्थ

Contract farming with good seeds, guaranteed prices, and direct crop purchase.

We work directly with farmers. Get pure seeds, crop guidance, guaranteed purchase price at harvest (हमीभाव), or sell your harvested crop directly.

What We Offer Farmers

Four simple promises to every farmer partner

1. Contract Farming (कॉन्ट्रॅक्ट फार्मिंग)

Partner with us before sowing: we provide pure seeds (especially Khapli wheat) and guarantee a fixed good purchase price when the crop is harvested, protecting you from market price drops.

2. Direct Crop Purchase (थेट माल खरेदी)

Any farmer can bring their harvested Khapli wheat, chana, moong, tur, soybean, bajra, or jowar directly to Shetimay. No middlemen, no broker cuts.

3. 100% Accurate Digital Weighing

Transparent weighing on computerized electronic scales. Accurate weight and direct bank transfer without delays or deductions.

4. Pure Seeds & Practical Advice

Get high-germination Khapli wheat seeds along with practical guidance on sowing, fertilizer, and water management.

Looking to buy grains in bulk or join contract farming?

Contact us for daily wholesale rates, samples, 50kg bags to truckloads, or farmer partnership.
