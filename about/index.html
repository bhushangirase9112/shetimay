🌱 Our Story & Purpose

Started by farmers, built on trust and honest trade.

Shetimay Farmer Producer Company was founded in Tawkheda (Dhule district, Maharashtra) with one clear mission: "शेतकऱ्यांच्या सेवार्थ" (In the service of farmers). We bring local farming families together, provide pure seeds, and ensure fair prices for their hard work.

The Khandesh region is known for rich black soil, perfect for nutritious Khapli wheat, pulses, and millets. Shetimay sources these pure crops directly from farms and supplies them to wholesale buyers, flour mills, and dal mills with strict quality checks.
